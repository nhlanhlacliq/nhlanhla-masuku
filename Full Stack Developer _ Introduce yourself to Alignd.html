Full Stack Developer : Introduce yourself to Alignd

Alignd is a value-based organism that brings together a unique combination of clinical, financial, and analytical skills with the aim of building a compassionate, human-centred, and sustainable health system. Our approach aligns the incentives of all stakeholders - patients, providers, and funders - to deliver human-centred, value-based healthcare.

Values

The Alignd team live out our core values daily - kindness, authenticity, right effort, interconnectedness and Yugma (unified collaboration). We emphasise the importance finding common ground and working together towards a common purpose, while still maintaining individual differences and uniqueness,  respecting diversity and promoting inclusivity.

The alignment with and demonstration of these values is a key consideration in making appointments.

The role 

We are looking for a Full-Stack Developer with a focus on front-end development and user experience. 

This role is crucial in enabling the vision of value-based care through designing, building, and maintaining the front end of human-centred applications. 

You will ensure that Alignd operations are supported and optimised by developing slick, intuitive, user-delighting, performant web applications, working in close partnership with our Senior Software Engineer, Solutions Architect and Back-End Developer. Collaboration with our users, responsiveness to user needs and a strong UX/UI capability are both essential for success in this role.
